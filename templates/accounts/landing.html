Good to know

A few things people ask.

Learn how PaperLoop supports the paper recovery process.

Get started with PaperLoop ↗
Who can use PaperLoop?

Educational institutions can list surplus paper, and recycling or collection partners can create profiles with their accepted materials and service areas. Organizations complete verification before role-specific access is granted.

How are recycler recommendations determined?

Recommendations consider listing and partner details such as accepted paper categories, service area, location and available capacity. Partners can review the match information before a collection request is sent.

Can both organizations follow a collection?

Yes. Collection requests move through request, acceptance, scheduling and completion stages. The related transaction record keeps the confirmed quantity connected to the collection.

Are the figures on this page estimates?

The platform figures shown above are calculated from completed transaction records and active verified recycler profiles. They are not projections of environmental savings.
