The idea behind the platform

Paper deserves a better next chapter.

Schools, colleges and universities handle paper in different forms and at different times. Finding a suitable recycler and coordinating a pickup can mean managing details across many conversations.

PaperLoop brings those steps into one place: institutions create listings, verified partners share their capabilities, and both sides can follow collection activity through a digital record.

03 / Recommendations with context

A match should make sense.

PaperLoop first checks whether a recycler is active, verified, accepting collections, able to handle the listed paper category, within the service area and able to accommodate the quantity.

Eligible partners are then ranked using available location, capacity and partner-history signals. When the location data needed by the learned ranker is unavailable, the platform uses its deterministic ranking path.

05 / Sustainability through traceability

A responsible loop begins with a reliable record.

PaperLoop connects a listing to its collection request and, when completed, to a transaction record. That creates a clearer account of the material moved through the platform.

The platform presents recorded quantities and collection outcomes. It does not turn them into unverified environmental savings.
